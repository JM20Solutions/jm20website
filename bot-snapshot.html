Murph — AI Support Agent

Murph isn't a FAQ bot. It reads your live database — orders, billing, inventory, cancellations, technical issues — and holds a real conversation until the problem is resolved. Type your message or just say it out loud — Murph transcribes and responds instantly. It remembers context across the full conversation. When it genuinely can't help, it doesn't drop the customer: it routes to a human with full conversation context, triggers a Slack alert, opens a dedicated escalation page, and fires acknowledgment and resolution emails automatically. The whole loop, closed.

  • Carries full conversations with memory — context, follow-ups, and reasoning, not one-shot answers.
  • Queries live data across orders, billing, inventory, cancellations and technical issues in one session.
  • Works on Web, WhatsApp & Telegram — wherever your customers already are.
  • Never traps a customer in a bot — offers to help first, then hands off to a human the moment they truly want one, with full context, Slack alert, form, and acknowledgment & resolution emails.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Murph handle our specific products, pricing and policies?

Yes — Murph connects directly to your database, so it knows your actual inventory, pricing, orders and billing in real time. It doesn't work from a static FAQ. You update your data, Murph knows about it instantly.

Do I need to migrate my database?

Never. We connect to your existing systems — MySQL, MongoDB, Airtable, Stripe, and more — through secure wrappers. Your data stays exactly where it is.

How is this different from other AI chatbots?

Most chatbots answer from a fixed script. Murph reasons over your live data — it can tell a customer their specific order is delayed because it was processed late, not just that 'delays may occur.' It carries context across the full conversation, and when it escalates, it hands off everything — not just a ticket number.

How long does implementation take?

Typically 2–4 weeks for a full setup including the support agent, secure architecture, and dashboard. Simpler setups can be live in under a week.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

Murph escalates — but not blindly. When it can't resolve something, it hands off to a human with the full conversation context, so your team picks up exactly where the AI left off. Simultaneously, it opens a dedicated escalation page, triggers a Slack alert, and sends the customer an acknowledgment email. When the issue is resolved, a resolution email goes out automatically. No customer repeats themselves. No thread goes cold.

What happens after we go live — are we on our own?

Not at all. The optional managed retainer covers monitoring of your n8n workflows, Supabase Edge Functions, LLM costs, and security checks. If something needs updating as your business evolves, we handle it. You own the system — we just make sure it keeps running perfectly.
